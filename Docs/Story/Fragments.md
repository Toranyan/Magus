# Fragments

## Overview

Fragments are isolated pieces of the world separated by magical boundaries.

From a gameplay perspective, a fragment represents an individual playable map.

From the perspective of the people living in the world, fragments are simply how the world exists.

---

# Stable Fragments

Stable fragments support permanent settlements and civilization.

Characteristics:

* Towns and cities
* Predictable environment
* Permanent structures
* Reliable access
* Safe enough for long-term habitation

Major story locations are generally stable fragments.

---

# Unstable Fragments

Unstable fragments are dangerous and unpredictable.

Characteristics:

* Monster populations
* Magical anomalies
* Valuable resources
* Ancient ruins
* Frequent environmental variation

These fragments form the primary gameplay loop and are often procedurally generated.

---

# Fragment Expeditions

Mages regularly travel into unstable fragments to:

* Eliminate threats.
* Investigate anomalies.
* Recover artifacts.
* Gather magical materials.
* Explore previously unknown locations.

Not every expedition is part of the main story.

---

# Fragment Threats

Although separated, fragments can sometimes threaten inhabited regions.

Examples include:

* Increased monster activity.
* Magical instability.
* Dangerous anomalies.
* Unknown phenomena.

These events often become the catalyst for story missions.

---

# Design Goals

Fragments exist to unify gameplay and narrative.

Instead of entering disconnected game levels, the player is exploring real locations that exist naturally within the world's geography.

Every map should feel like another place within the same world.

---
