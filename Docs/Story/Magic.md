# Magic


## Philosophy

Magic is the study of how Nothing becomes Everything.

Mages manipulate the six elemental forces that compose reality.



---

# Fundamental Elements

The world's magic is built upon six primary elements arranged as opposing pairs.

* Fire ↔ Ice
* Wind ↔ Earth
* Light ↔ Dark

These represent the foundation of all known magic.

---

# Specialization

Most mages naturally specialize in one or two elements.

Mastering additional elements becomes increasingly difficult.


---

# Composite Magic

When elemental knowledge overlaps, new spells become possible.

Examples may include:

* Fire + Earth
* Wind + Ice
* Light + Fire
* Dark + Earth


---



# Relationship to Gameplay

The player begins with only basic magical knowledge.

Exploration, combat, and discovery gradually expand the player's understanding, unlocking new spells and magical combinations.

Story progression and gameplay progression are intended to complement one another.
