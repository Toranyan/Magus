# Story Overview

## High Concept

**Magus** is a fantasy action roguelite set in a world that has been broken into countless magical **Fragments**.

The player takes the role of a young mage who wants to discover who she is.

Fragments serve as both the foundation of the world's geography and the game's core gameplay loop. Each expedition takes the player into another fragment, where they face monsters, recover magical artifacts, and gradually reveal pieces of the world's history.

As the story progresses, the player uncovers a mystery far larger than individual fragment incursions, eventually learning why the world became fragmented in the first place.

It is also connected to who she is.


---

# Premise

Long ago, the world became divided into isolated landmasses known as **Fragments**.

Most of civilization now lives on one or more large stable fragments.

Outside these safe regions exist countless unstable fragments containing monsters, ruins, forgotten civilizations, magical resources, and dangerous phenomena.

Some fragments become threats to inhabited regions, requiring skilled mages to investigate, stabilize, or eliminate the danger.
