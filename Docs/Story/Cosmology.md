# Cosmology

> *"Everything is Nothing. Nothing is Everything."*


---

# The Nature of Nothing

Most people believe "Nothing" means the absence of existence.

The oldest magical traditions teach otherwise.

Nothing is not emptiness.

Nothing is possibility.

Everything that has ever existed, or ever could exist, originates from Nothing.

Likewise, all things eventually return to Nothing.

Nothing and Everything are not opposites.

They are simply two ways of viewing the same truth.

---

# Creation

The six elemental forces are understood to be the fundamental building blocks of reality.

* Fire
* Ice
* Wind
* Earth
* Light
* Dark

Everything within the world is composed of these elements in different relationships.

The greatest unanswered question among mages is what lies beyond the six elements.

Some believe perfect balance between all elements returns matter to Nothing.

Others believe Nothing itself gave rise to the six elements.

No one has proven either theory.

---

# Fragments

Fragments are manifestations of reality.

Whether they were created, discovered, or imagined remains unknown.

Each fragment possesses its own landscape, creatures, history, and people.

To those who live within them, their world is no less real than any other.

---

# Consciousness

Scholars have long debated whether beings created from Nothing possess true consciousness.

There is no consensus.

Fragments contain people who:

* Think.
* Learn.
* Dream.
* Love.
* Grieve.
* Fear death.
* Make choices.

If these qualities do not define life, then what does?


---

# The Protagonist

Unknown to the protagonist, her search for her identity and her search for the truth of the fragments are the same journey.

As she uncovers the nature of the world, she is forced to confront a terrifying possibility:

If this world was imagined...

Does that make it any less real?

Or does reality begin the moment someone is capable of living within it?

---

# Central Themes

## Everything is Nothing. Nothing is Everything.

Creation and destruction are not opposites.

Nothing is not the absence of existence, but the source of all possibility.

The world, its people, and perhaps even the protagonist herself are expressions of this truth.

---

## What Makes Something Real?

Throughout her journey, the protagonist encounters people, places, and experiences that feel undeniably real.

Yet as she uncovers the true nature of the world, she is forced to question whether reality is determined by origin or by experience.

If someone can think, love, fear, dream, and choose...

Are they any less real because they were imagined?

Likewise, if a world can inspire joy, grief, hope, and sacrifice...

Does it matter how that world came to exist?

The story deliberately leaves these questions unanswered, inviting the player to decide for themselves.
