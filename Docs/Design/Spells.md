# Spells

## Tier 1 Philosophy

Tier 1 spells teach the fundamentals of an element.

They should:

* Be easy to understand.
* Have clear combat applications.
* Demonstrate elemental identity.
* Support interactions with other elements.
* Remain useful throughout the game.

Tier 1 spells should not be simple starter spells that become obsolete.

They are foundational tools.

---

## Spell Categories

Every element contains:

* 1 Offensive spell
* 1 Defensive spell
* 1 Support spell

Total:

6 Elements × 3 Categories = 18 Tier 1 Spells

This ensures players can access offense, defense, and utility regardless of elemental preference.

---

## Design Goals

Each spell should answer:

* What problem does this solve?
* What opportunity does this create?
* What interaction does this enable?

Avoid creating spells that only deal damage.

Avoid creating spells that only apply a status.

Every spell should contribute to decision making.
