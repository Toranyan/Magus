# Strategic Triangle

Combat is built around three strategic categories.

Every spell belongs primarily to one category.

## Offense

Creates threats.

Examples:

* Direct damage
* Damage over time
* Burst damage
* Area denial
* Pressure

Offense defeats Support.

Support actions often sacrifice immediate power for future value or utility.

## Defense

Negates threats.

Examples:

* Shields
* Barriers
* Deflection
* Protection
* Damage reduction

Defense defeats Offense.

Defensive actions efficiently answer direct threats.

## Support

Creates opportunities.

Examples:

* Mana generation
* Buffs
* Debuffs
* Cleansing
* Repositioning
* Status application
* Battlefield manipulation

Support defeats Defense.

Defensive actions lose value when no immediate threat exists.

---

# Strategic Relationship

Offense > Support

Defense > Offense

Support > Defense

No category should dominate.

Combat should constantly shift between all three.
