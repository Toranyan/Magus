# Elements

## Overview

The game contains six primary elements.

Every element contains:

* Offense spells
* Defense spells
* Support spells

However, each element naturally favors one strategic category.

Elements exist in opposing pairs.

* Fire ↔ Water
* Earth ↔ Wind
* Light ↔ Dark

Opposing elements should feel philosophically opposed rather than existing as simple elemental weaknesses.

---

# Fire

## Tendency

Offense

## Opposes

Water

## Fantasy

Fire represents heat, aggression, destruction, and expansion.

Fire seeks to overwhelm situations through pressure and force.

## Themes

* Burning
* Explosions
* Heat
* Aggression
* Area denial
* Escalation

## Strengths

* Creating pressure
* Punishing passive play
* Threat generation

## Weaknesses

* Direct protection
* Resource efficiency

# Design Rules

Every element must support:

* Offense
* Defense
* Support

An element's tendency influences its identity but does not limit its capabilities.

Players should be able to create viable builds centered around any element.

The purpose of elements is to encourage different approaches to solving combat situations rather than creating rigid strengths and weaknesses.
