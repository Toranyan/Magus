# Magus

## Elevator Pitch

A third-person action roguelite where the player is a mage.

The core gameplay revolves around countering enemy attacks with the correct spell. Success comes from understanding magical interactions and enemy behaviors rather than pure reaction speed.

## Design Pillars

### Countering Feels Powerful

The player should feel rewarded for correctly reading enemy intentions and responding with the appropriate spell.

### Knowledge Over Execution

Success should come primarily from understanding systems and interactions.

Mechanical skill should help but should not be the primary determinant of victory.

## Player Fantasy

The player is not a wizard throwing random fireballs.

The player is a skilled mage who understands magical principles and can manipulate them to overcome challenges.

The player should feel clever.

## Scope

Current target is a playable vertical slice that demonstrates:

* Core movement
* Spell preparation system
* Tier 1 spells
* Counter mechanics
* Multiple enemy archetypes
* Roguelite progression

The project should remain small enough for a solo developer to complete.

## Inspirations

* Action roguelites
* Character action games
* Spellcrafting systems
* Games that reward knowledge and mastery
